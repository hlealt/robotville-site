second-brain

A private personal assistant by Robotville

second-brain is a personal productivity assistant used by one person, Henrique Teixeira, for his own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the app does

With the owner's permission, second-brain uses Google APIs to:

  • read Google Meet and Google Drive meeting transcripts and produce meeting summaries;
  • file those summaries and related notes into the owner's private notes;
  • read and draft Gmail messages and read and manage Google Calendar events on the owner's behalf.

All processing happens on the owner's own private server, for the owner only.
